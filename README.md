🛍️ Ecommerce Dashboard
Short Description / Purpose
The Ecommerce Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to track and analyze key ecommerce performance metrics across multiple dimensions such as brand, platform, store, product category, and discount code. It enables stakeholders to monitor total sales, customer acquisition, and average order value trends over time for data-driven decision-making.
🧰 Tech Stack
Power BI Desktop – Core data visualization platform.
Power Query – Used for data cleaning, integration, and preparation.
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – For calculating KPIs such as AOV, new/returning customer revenue, and customer counts.
Data Modeling – Established relationships between customer, order, and product tables to enable dynamic filtering.
File Format – .pbix for dashboard file and .png for dashboard preview.
📂 Data Source
Source: Internal ecommerce transaction data containing orders, products, customers, and discount details.
The dataset includes:
- ~500 total orders
- Customer segments (New vs. Returning)
- Product categories, platforms, and stores
- Order-level details with revenue, quantity, and discount information
🌟 Features / Highlights
Business Problem
Ecommerce teams often struggle to monitor performance across multiple dimensions such as customer type, brand, and product category. Manual analysis of customer retention, AOV, and revenue contributions can be time-consuming and fragmented.
Goal of the Dashboard
To create a unified and interactive report that:
- Tracks key ecommerce metrics (Revenue, Orders, AOV, New vs. Returning Customers)
- Visualizes performance trends over time
- Enables comparison across brands, platforms, and stores

💡 Business Impact & Insights
- Customer Retention Tracking: Identifies how much revenue is driven by returning vs. new customers.
- Revenue Growth Analysis: Highlights the contribution of top-performing brands and stores.
- Performance Monitoring: Enables stakeholders to track order and revenue trends by product category and time period.
- Data-Driven Marketing: Supports discount and campaign optimization by analyzing brand-level AOV and order behavior.
